SENTINEL AI

The Waze for Crime.

An AI platform that predicts and prevents urban crime before it happens — not after. It reads the live city, learns where and when crime clusters, and sends the nearest patrol to the right place in time.

See how it works View the code
The problem

Cities are full of cameras.
Crime still wins.

We already have the hardware. We use it the wrong way — to look backwards, in isolation, after the fact.

Cameras only record

By the time anyone reviews the footage, the crime is already over. CCTV is a witness, not a guard.

Data lives in silos

Police records, CCTV feeds and transit data never talk to each other — so no one sees the full picture.

Response is reactive

Patrols are dispatched after a 999 call. Help arrives once the damage is done.

But crime is predictable

It clusters in the same districts, at the same hours. That pattern is exactly what an AI can learn.

The solution

One closed loop that learns.

SentinelAI is a five-layer intelligence loop. Cameras analyse on-device and send only metadata — never raw video — so it is fast, private and light on bandwidth.

The engine

Three kinds of intelligence,
fused into one.

Most systems answer a single question. SentinelAI answers all three at once — what is happening, where it clusters, and when risk rises.

Real-time vision
SEE

What is happening now

YOLOv8 and ByteTrack detect people, bags, vehicles and weapons on the live feed — and read behaviours like running, chasing, loitering and fighting.

HDBSCAN clustering
WHERE

Where crime clusters

Learns the city's real hotspots from three years of crime data — finding natural, irregular shapes instead of forcing neat circles onto a messy map.

SARIMA forecasting
WHEN

When risk will rise

Learns the rhythm of crime — night versus day, weekends, festivals — and forecasts where risk will climb in the next 15 to 30 minutes.

The core idea

Weak signals become
one trustworthy alarm.

A person running means little on its own. Running and being chased and a bag-snatch at night is an emergency. SentinelAI fuses weak clues into one explainable risk score.

Monitoring
Replay the fusion

R_zone = 0.4 · P_base + 0.6 · P_realtime. Every signal fades on its own timer, so there are no permanently-red zones and no false-alarm spam — the risk map stays in step with the real city.

From risk to response

It does not just watch.
It acts.

H3

The city becomes hexagons

SentinelAI tiles the city with Uber's H3 grid. Every cell has six equal neighbours, so coverage and distance maths stay consistent and fair.

02

A zone crosses the threshold

When fused risk gets high enough, the cell turns red and an incident is created automatically — no operator required.

03

The nearest patrol is dispatched

It finds the closest available unit, computes distance and ETA, and recommends the dispatch — in under 30 seconds from detection.
