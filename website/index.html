The Waze for Crime.

The problem

Cameras are everywhere.
Crime still wins.

Cities are covered in CCTV, yet street crime keeps happening. The technology we already have is being used the wrong way — to look backwards.

📼

Cameras only record

By the time anyone reviews the footage, the crime is already over. CCTV is a witness, not a guard.

🧩

Data lives in silos

Police records, CCTV feeds and transit data never talk to each other — so no one sees the full picture.

⏱️

Response is reactive

Patrols are dispatched after a 999 call. Help arrives once the damage is done.

📈

But crime is predictable

It clusters in the same districts, at the same hours. That pattern is exactly what an AI can learn.

The solution

One closed loop that learns.

SentinelAI isn't a CCTV add-on. It's a five-layer intelligence loop — cameras think locally and send only metadata (never raw video), so it's fast, private, and cheap on bandwidth.

The brain

Three kinds of intelligence,
fused into one.

Most systems answer just one question. SentinelAI answers all three at once — what's happening, where it tends to happen, and when.

SEE
Real-time vision

What's happening now

YOLOv8 + ByteTrack spot people, bags, vehicles and weapons on the live feed — and read behaviours like running, chasing, loitering and fighting.

WHERE
HDBSCAN hotspots

Where crime clusters

Learns the city's real hotspots from 3 years of crime data — finding irregular, natural shapes instead of forcing neat circles on a messy map.

WHEN
SARIMA forecast

When risk will rise

Learns the rhythm of crime — night vs day, weekends, festivals — and forecasts where risk will climb in the next 15–30 minutes.

The core idea

Weak signals → one trustworthy alarm.

A person running means nothing on its own. But running and being chased and a bag-snatch at night — that's an emergency. SentinelAI fuses weak clues into one explainable risk score.

▶ Replay the fusion

R_zone = 0.4·P_base + 0.6·P_realtime. Each signal fades on its own timer, so there are no permanently-red zones and no false-alarm spam — the risk map breathes with the city in real time.

From risk to response

It doesn't just watch.
It acts.

H3

The city becomes hexagons

SentinelAI tiles Kuala Lumpur with Uber's H3 grid — every cell has 6 equal neighbours, so coverage maths stays clean and fair.

⚠

A zone crosses the threshold

When fused risk gets high enough, the cell turns red and an incident is created automatically.

🚓

The nearest patrol is dispatched

It finds the closest available unit, calculates distance and ETA, and recommends the dispatch — in under 30 seconds from detection.
